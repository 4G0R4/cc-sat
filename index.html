⚠ How This Works
Price Definition: All prices are gross rates — what you pay per CC before the 30% sybil fee. Learn more about SN fees.
Example (Fees Included): If you buy 1,000 CC at 0.6 sats/CC with fees included → You pay 600 sats, receive 700 CC (30% deducted).
Example (Buyer Pays): If you buy 1,000 CC at 0.6 sats/CC with buyer paying fees → You pay 600 sats + 180 sats fee = 780 sats, receive 1,000 CC.
Territory Founders: Receive 21% rebate when trading in their territory, allowing better rates. Look for the Founder badge.
